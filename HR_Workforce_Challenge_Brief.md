# HR Workforce Planning & Attrition Analytics Challenge

> Build an interactive Power BI report that helps HR and business leaders understand workforce growth, attrition, headcount planning, and employee retention risks.

---

## Challenge Brief

In this **ZoomCharts Power BI Challenge**, participants will work with an HR workforce planning dataset covering:

- Employee profiles
- Monthly headcount snapshots
- Hiring and termination events
- Compensation data
- Performance reviews
- Engagement surveys
- Absences
- Recruitment activity
- Workforce targets

The goal is to design a **clear, decision-ready HR analytics report** that helps stakeholders:

- Compare the current workforce against planned targets
- Identify attrition patterns
- Understand where people-related risks may affect future workforce planning

## Report Objectives

Your report should help stakeholders answer the following questions:

### 📈 Workforce Growth
- How has headcount and FTE changed over time?

### ⚠️ Attrition Risk
- Which departments, locations, roles, or employee segments show the highest attrition?

### 🔍 Termination Reasons
- What are the main termination reasons?
- How do voluntary and involuntary exits differ?

### 🤝 Retention Drivers
- How do engagement, performance, absence, tenure, and compensation patterns relate to retention?

### 🎯 Workforce Targets
- Where are actual workforce levels above or below planned headcount, FTE, or salary targets?

### 🧲 Recruitment Support
- How effectively is recruitment supporting workforce needs?

---

## Design Focus

Combine **strong HR storytelling** with **interactive Power BI report design**.

Use **ZoomCharts Drill Down visuals** to make it easy to:

- Explore workforce trends
- Compare departments and employee segments
- Move from high-level HR KPIs into detailed attrition and planning insights
